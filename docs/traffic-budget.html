How this is estimated

Measured in v148.9, including the extra reads Firestore’s security rules make: about 14 reads and 6 writes per ranked battle (score, coins, progress); about 22 reads per leaderboard visit (the top 20 rows; re-opening within a minute is free); about 3 reads plus one per started territory for each Adventure map visit; about 12 reads / 5 writes per finished operation; about 6 reads / 3 writes per recruitment pull; plus around 24 reads and 3 writes for signing in and moving between screens. Teacher pages, reconnects and busy leaderboards add a little more. The free quota is shared by everyone using your Firebase project and resets each day at midnight Pacific time (5 pm in Japan, or 4 pm during US daylight saving time).
