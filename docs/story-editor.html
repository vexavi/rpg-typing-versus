    Battle Music

    Reuse the same BGM in any stage.

    Upload a track under Stage → Battle Settings → Battle Music. Click Download Music Pack, extract into the GitHub repository root, commit/push, then use Check Music before Publish to Students. Tracks go to docs/story/audio/. To reuse a track already on GitHub, click Find Existing Music, then choose it from a stage. Supports MP3, M4A, WEBM/Opus, OGG and WAV. Use the GitHub repository entered under Chapter Images above.

    Music Report

      Chapter Images, Shared Boss Sprites & Battle Backgrounds + Music

      Under Battle Settings, choose Use Existing Image to reuse a boss sprite or battle background from another stage. A shared image is uploaded only once. To create a new one, click Upload New Boss Sprite or Upload New Battle Background; these use short shared filenames. On a slide, click Select Photo to choose an image from your computer. The preview is local. For new images only, click Download Photo Pack, extract it into your GitHub repository root (so files land in docs/story/images/), then commit and push. If your photos are already in GitHub, click Find Existing Images at the top instead of uploading them again. After Check Images reports that the files are online, click Publish to Students. This avoids paid Firebase Cloud Storage. JPG, PNG, and WEBP are supported; photos are automatically optimized to WEBP when your browser supports it.

      Music files are not stored in the browser draft: download the Music Pack before reloading. Browser autoplay restrictions can require students to tap a control or unmute audio. Player progress is tied to stage IDs. IDs are fixed once created; changing stage order or deleting published stages can change which stages unlock. Save Draft is local to this browser and does not save image bytes: download the Photo Pack of any newly selected images before refreshing or closing this tab. Existing GitHub-hosted images do not need a new Photo Pack. Publishing requires your authorized teacher Google account and an added Firestore rule.
